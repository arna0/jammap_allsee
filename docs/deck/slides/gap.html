Today's maps only see the sky

COVERED TODAY

Airways at cruise altitude

Public interference maps are built from aircraft position reports. Where planes don't fly, there is no data.

NOT COVERED

The ground

Drones, trucks, tractors and delivery vehicles all steer by GPS. None of them get a warning before it fails.

03

Existing interference maps come from aircraft: planes broadcast how accurate their position is, and services plot where it drops. That only covers airways. On the ground, where drones fly at 100 meters and trucks and tractors drive, nobody is measuring. An agricultural drone or a delivery fleet has no way to know an area is jammed until something goes wrong.
