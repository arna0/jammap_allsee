02

GPS jamming and spoofing are no longer rare. According to IATA, spoofing grew 193 percent and jamming 67 percent between 2023 and 2025. At the 2024 peak, spoofing hit up to 1,500 flights a day. Jamming blocks the satellite signal. Spoofing is worse: it feeds the receiver a fake position, and the device believes it.
