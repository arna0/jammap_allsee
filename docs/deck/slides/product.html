This is the product running today. On the left, the live map: red is a confirmed jamming zone, purple is spoofing, dashed boxes are reports still waiting for more phones. On the right, the evidence for one incident: every dot is one phone, and they all sit in the jamming corner, signal down and gain down. Customers can also check a route before a flight. Everything here was built during the hackathon: the Solana program, the relayer and paid API, the Android app and the dashboard.
