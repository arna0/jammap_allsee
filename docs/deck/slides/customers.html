Who pays for this? Drone operators, because losing GPS means a crash or a flyaway, so they check the route before every flight. Precision farming, because tractors and sprayers steer by GPS. Fleet tracking companies, who see gaps and fake positions in truck tracks. And insurers, who need proof of whether GPS failed at a specific time and place. Pricing is per request for software, and a monthly plan for alerts and history. Part of the revenue goes back to the phones as rewards.
