Here is the insight. Billions of Android phones already contain a GNSS receiver, and Android lets apps read raw satellite measurements. Two numbers matter. Signal-to-noise per satellite, and the receiver's automatic gain. When someone jams, noise floods the antenna: signal-to-noise drops and the receiver turns its gain down. When a phone is just in a pocket, signal also drops, but the receiver turns the gain up. That difference is what lets us avoid false alarms.
